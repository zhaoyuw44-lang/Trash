Polyurethane Synthesis Bench

Make a segmented thermoplastic polyurethane by the two-step prepolymer route. Dry the polyol, cap it with a diisocyanate under catalysis, then chain-extend with a diol. Every reagent, solvent, catalyst and piece of glassware is on the bench, and the flask, the thermometer, the FTIR and the molecular weight respond to what you do.

    Safety, waste and the model

    Safety

    • Diisocyanates are respiratory sensitizers. A single over-exposure can cause lifelong asthma. Handle them only in a fume hood, with nitrile or butyl gloves, goggles and a lab coat.
    • DMAc and DMF are reproductive toxicants that pass through skin. Change gloves at once after a splash.
    • DBTDL is toxic and harmful to aquatic life. Dispense it as a dilute stock solution so you never handle neat milligram amounts.
    • Never heat a closed system. Keep the N₂ line vented through the oil bubbler, and release vacuum before you remove a stopper.
    • Methanol and THF are highly flammable. Keep them away from the hotplate surface.

    Waste and clean-up

    • Quench leftover isocyanate in a decontamination solution (for example 5–10 % sodium carbonate and 0.5 % detergent in water) for 48 h before disposal. Leave the container loosely capped, because it releases CO₂.
    • Collect DMAc, DMF and THF filtrates as non-halogenated organic waste.
    • Collect tin-containing residues separately as heavy-metal waste.
    • Soak glassware in the decontamination solution before washing. Polymer films come off with warm DMAc.

    How the simulation works

    • Second-order kinetics for NCO + OH and NCO + H₂O, with Arrhenius temperature dependence. Rate constants are illustrative values chosen to reproduce typical lab time scales, not measured data.
    • Molecular weight comes from counting molecules: every link formed removes one molecule (Carothers). Đ is taken as 1 + p for a linear step-growth polymer.
    • Viscosity uses the Martin equation with a Mark–Houwink estimate of intrinsic viscosity.
    • Allophanate branching switches on above about 110 °C. Gelation is flagged once there is more than one branch point per chain.
